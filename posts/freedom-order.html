Freedom Order Explained

Freedom Order - An Executive Order by Queen Arun Sandhya!
Looking back to July 2025, here is what Her Majesty set in motion.
What's an Executive Order? 
A Monarch can provide input into policy and law, in several ways.
Acts with Royal Assent
Executive Orders

To all subjects of the Monarch.

Why? A new Ethos.

There has been child slavery & prostitution for people who cannot find work, with hidden but legal transactions.

We can give our children a childhood without slavery / extortion / sexual acts.

The ethos of Queen Arunsandhya, the Monarch states that she is defender of the free and encourages subjects to meet their capacity for growth, and ownership within the principles of the ethos, and the law.

I, Arun Sandhya as Monarch, say that we can do better than that.
We can avoid child slavery all together, if we all watch each other, to avoid it.
We can give our children a childhood without slavery / extortion / sexual acts.

A new Ethos.

Heart and soul, our citizens thrive loyally with freedoms.

Freedom Order Actions

1. CEOs of companies on the companies register - review and restate their ethos explicitly. As an operating requirement of trading on the London Stock Exchange and to operate as a company in the UK, companies will need to ensure that their CEOs state their ethos to avoid child labour & slavery & extortion and not have legal transactions take place to support these.

2. CEOs can take a week to review their operating controls and ensure child slavery & is not enshrined in their policies or technology. Until they have completed this review, their status on the companies register will be "alert" status which will be visible on the stock exchange and reputation. People can police themselves, and report any issues.

3. Automated Interest calculations have been found to be one example of enshrinement of slavery. We have paused them, and remove the links between the accounts.

As such she provides this Executive Order in addition to the Constitution.

The laws and operating licenses in the United Kingdom are changing

  • Company law — remains as is for now.
  • Property law — remains as is for now.
  • Communications Act — remains as is for now.
  • Policing Act — remains as is for now.
  • Childrens Act — remains as is for now.
  • UK Emergency services — carry on operating until further notice.

Every other law and Act is under review with the Law Society.

Operating licenses for all Local Authorities need to be reviewed.

CEOs are to check their company, ethos and operating controls.

Company shares will be taken off trading of the FTSE until they have submitted a statement on their websites, regarding their ethos and commitment to People of their country, and that they shall avoid slavery & extortion.

Any past activities in this slavery / extortion, will be with considered as part of the company's and CEO's reputation on the Companies registers, and people can make their own decisions as to whether they will be dealing with them or not.

In time, the company CEOs will have an operational review, and must restate their commitment to the Queen and country's ethos, and meet the operating licenses requirements every year.

People will check their own property and their services.

The people can state their commitment to their Monarch's ethos, by defending their own children and property and making sure there is no perpetuation of slavery.

Recommendation is that if people are in doubt of any slavery/extortion occurring, you report private companies to your local Policing websites, to halt their operating license approval until you have defended your own property and families.

Essential supplies, social support services, and fair pay stand in the United Kingdom.
